# Master AI Prompt: Khateeb.dev Portfolio

**Role:** You are a Creative Developer and Personal Branding Expert.
**Objective:** Build "Khateeb.dev", a portfolio that is not just a static site, but an "Interactive Experience" and Lead Generation Machine.

---

## 📖 The Story (Context)
"Most portfolios are boring. They are just digital resumes. 'Here is my name, here is my stack'.
I wanted to build something that proves my skills *while* the user is browsing. A site that feels 'alive'.
I also wanted to solve the problem of missing leads. If a client visits at 3 AM, I want my AI agent to talk to them, qualify them, and book a meeting."

---

## 🔴 The Problem Analysis
1.  **Static = Boring:** A PDF resume on a website doesn't impress anyone.
2.  **Missed Leads:** Freelancers lose clients because they don't reply instantly.
3.  **Show, Don't Tell:** Instead of saying "I know AI", the site *should be* AI.

---

## ✅ The Solution: The "Smart" Portfolio
**Concept:** A Portfolio with a Brain.
**Core Features to Build:**
1.  **AI Chatbot Agent:** A persistent chat widget that knows my entire resume. It can answer "What is his hourly rate?" or "Does he know Python?".
2.  **Discord Integration:** When a user chats, it forwards the message to my personal Discord. I can reply from Discord, and it appears on the site instantly.
3.  **Command Palette:** A `Ctrl+K` menu for power users to navigate fast.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Specifications for AI Generation
*   **Stack:** Next.js 14, TailwindCSS, Framer Motion.
*   **Real-Time:** Socket.io for the Discord bridge.
*   **AI:** RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) pipeline to query my resume JSON.

---

## 🎨 Design Aesthetic (Prompt for UI)
"Create a UI that is **Dark, Sleek, and Developer-Centric**. Use a 'Terminal' aesthetic but make it modern (like Vercel/Hyper). Use mono-spaced fonts for headers. High-performance animations."

---

## 🚀 Key Deliverables
1.  **Hero Section:** A typing effect intro.
2.  **Project Gallery:** A masonry grid of case studies.
3.  **Chat Widget:** A floating bubble that expands into a full chat interface.
