# Master AI Prompt: AID²E Islamic Ecosystem

**Role:** You are a Social Impact Architect and Blockchain Developer.
**Objective:** Build/Simulate "AID²E", a Digital Operating System for Islamic Institutions (Masjids/Madrasas) focused on Transparency.

---

## 📖 The Story (Context)
"The Muslim world donates billions in charity (Zakat/Sadaqah), but there is a massive 'Trust Deficit'. Donors don't know if their money actually reached the poor.
Meanwhile, Masjids and Madrasas are stuck in the 19th century—using paper registers for attendance and cash for donations. This lack of digitization leads to inefficiency and fraud.
We wanted to build a platform that brings 'Radical Transparency' to Islamic Finance and modernizes religious education."

---

## 🔴 The Problem Analysis
1.  **The Black Box of Charity:** You give $100 to a charity. You never hear about it again.
2.  **Inefficiency:** Madrasas spend hours manually counting cash fees and taking roll-call.
3.  **Youth Disconnect:** Young people live on their phones. If the Masjid isn't digital, it loses relevance.

---

## ✅ The Solution: AID²E
**Concept:** A "Smart Masjid" Ecosystem.
**Core Features to Build:**
1.  **Proof of Donation:** When a donor gives money, they get a tracking ID. When the money is spent (e.g., buying food), the admin uploads a photo/receipt. The donor gets a notification: "Your $100 bought 5 bags of rice. See photo."
2.  **Digital Madrasa:** An app for parents to see their child's attendance and Quran progress in real-time.
3.  **Smart Zakat:** An AI calculator that connects directly to verified beneficiaries.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Specifications for AI Generation
*   **Transparency:** Use an immutable ledger (or just a very secure Audit Log) to track every cent.
*   **Mobile First:** The primary users are parents and donors on phones. Build with Flutter or React Native.
*   **Backend:** Node.js Microservices.

---

## 🎨 Design Aesthetic (Prompt for UI)
"Create a UI that blends **Tradition with Modernity**. Use Islamic geometric patterns (Arabesque) subtly in the background, but keep the interface clean and modern (Material Design). Use Green and Gold accents."

---

## 🚀 Key Deliverables
1.  **Donor Dashboard:** A feed showing "Where my money went" with photos.
2.  **Madrasa Admin:** A tablet view for teachers to mark attendance.
3.  **Zakat Calculator:** A wizard to calculate assets and pay instantly.
