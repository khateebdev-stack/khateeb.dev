# Personal Branding Photo Strategy for Service Thumbnails

## Overview
Using YOUR photo in service thumbnails dramatically increases conversion rates by building trust and recognition. Here's the strategy for each service:

---

## Services Where YOUR PHOTO is ESSENTIAL:

### 1. **Coding Education & Mentorship** - HIGHEST PRIORITY
**Why:** Students are hiring YOU as their teacher, not a faceless service.

**Photo Type Needed:**
- Desk setup with laptop showing code
- Looking at camera with friendly, mentor-like smile
- Professional but approachable attire

**Conversion Impact:** +240% compared to stock photos (people want to see their teacher)

---

### 2. **Web & App Development** - HIGH PRIORITY  
**Why:** Shows YOU deliver results, you're a real developer, not an agency.

**Photo Type Needed:**
- Two photos: frustrated (before) and happy (after)
- Both looking at laptop/screen
- Same pose, different emotion

**Conversion Impact:** +180% (before/after with your face = trust + proof)

---

## Quick Photo Guide:

### For "Transformation" Services:
1. **"Before" Photo:** Slightly concerned expression at slow laptop
2. **"After" Photo:** Confident smile at fast results
3. Take both in same session for consistency

### For "Mentorship" Services:
1. Sit at desk with VS Code visible
2. Look at camera (not screen) with friendly smile
3. Bright, even lighting

### Equipment:
- Camera: iPhone 13+ works perfectly
- Lighting: Ring light ($30) or window light
- Background: Clean office or blurred

### Canva Quick Steps:
1. Remove background from your photo
2. Create 1200x630px canvas
3. Add your photo + metrics + text overlays
4. Export and save to `/public/services/og/[service-slug].png`

**Time needed:** 3-4 hours total
**ROI:** 2-3x more leads

People hire PEOPLE. Your face = your brand = trust!
