## 1. Web & App Development

### PRIMARY PROMPT - Transformation Focus (WITH YOUR PHOTO)
```
Professional business banner, 16:9 aspect ratio. Split screen design:

LEFT SIDE (40%): Photo of YOU (headshot_v5.png in/public/marketing) looking frustrated/concerned at slow laptop showing "Loading..." spinner and "Bounce Rate: 67%". Muted grey/red tones. Professional attire.

RIGHT SIDE (60%): Photo of YOU (same person) now smiling confidently, looking at fast modern dashboard with satisfied expression. 

BOLD TEXT OVERLAYS (must be clearly readable):
- Top right corner: "BEFORE → AFTER"
- Center right, large: "3X FASTER"
- Below that: "50% More Sales"
- Small badge bottom: "Real Client Results"
- Bottom left corner: "by Khateeb" or your logo

Color scheme: Left is desaturated, Right is vibrant blue/green. Professional photography style, high contrast for mobile readability.
```

**How to Generate WITH Your Photo:**

**Method 1 - Midjourney Image Prompting:**
1. Take 2 professional photos of yourself:
   - Photo 1: Neutral/concerned expression, looking at laptop (good lighting)
   - Photo 2: Happy/confident expression, looking at screen
2. Upload both to Discord
3. In Midjourney, use:
```
/imagine [your photo 1 URL] frustrated developer looking at slow laptop, "Loading..." on screen, muted colors, professional office --ar 16:9
```
4. Repeat for photo 2 with "confident" and vibrant colors
5. Combine in Canva or Photoshop as split screen

**Method 2 - Canva (Easiest):**
1. Use Canva's "Background Remover" on your professional photo
2. Create 1200x630px canvas
3. Left side: Your photo + laptop mockup + "Loading..." + muted filter
4. Right side: Your photo (same pose but brighter) + dashboard + metrics
5. Add text overlays as specified

**Method 3 - Professional Photoshoot (Best Quality):**
1. Hire photographer for 1-hour session ($50-150)
2. Get shots of you:
   - Looking frustrated at slow computer
   - Looking happy at fast results
   - Both with laptop visible, professional background
3. Edit in Photoshop to add metrics/text

**Why Use YOUR Photo:**
✅ **Personal Brand Recognition** - People connect with faces, not logos
✅ **Trust Building** - Shows you're a real person, not an agency
✅ **Memorability** - They'll remember "that developer who showed results"
✅ **LinkedIn Consistency** - Matches your profile photo = stronger brand

**Post-Generation Checklist:**
✓ Can you read "3X FASTER" on a phone screen in 2 seconds?
✓ Is the "After" side significantly brighter/more appealing?
✓ Does it clearly show business software (not generic code)?
✓ Do both photos look professional (good lighting, crisp quality)?
✓ Is your face clearly visible in both sides?

## UNIVERSAL GENERATION TIPS

### Text Readability Rules:
1. **Contrast Minimum**: Text must be readable when zoomed to 400x300px (mobile preview)
2. **Font Size Hierarchy**:
   - Main headline: 80-120px equivalent
   - Metrics: 60-80px
   - Supporting text: 40-50px
3. **Background Treatment**: 
   - White text needs dark overlay (70% opacity black box)
   - Dark text needs light overlay or solid white area

### Tool-Specific Instructions:

**MIDJOURNEY:**
```
[Your prompt] --ar 16:9 --stylize 250 --v 6 --no watermark, signature
```
- Add `--no text` if AI mangles text, then add text in Canva
- Use `--stylize 100` for more literal, less artistic results

**DALL-E 3:**
- It handles text better - be VERY specific about text content
- Use: "Professional sans-serif bold font, high contrast"
- Specify exact text: 'The text must say exactly "3X FASTER"'

**CANVA (For Text Overlays):**
1. Import AI-generated background (without text)
2. Add text boxes with:
   - Font: Montserrat Bold or Poppins Bold
   - Shadow: 50% opacity, 4px offset
   - Background: Semi-transparent box if needed

### Quality Checklist (Before Using):
✅ Can I read the main value prop in 2 seconds?
✅ Is there a clear number/metric visible?
✅ Does it show transformation or results?
✅ Would I click this over a competitor's generic image?
✅ Does it work when cropped to square (WhatsApp)?

### Platform Optimization:

**LinkedIn:**
- Focus on corporate metrics
- Professional color schemes
- Include job titles or company types

**Twitter/X:**
- Bolder claims
- Bigger contrast
- Shorter text phrases

**WhatsApp:**  
- Test in square crop (center 630x630px)
- Simpler designs (viewed on small screens)
- Warmer, more personal tone

## Quick Start Formula:

Every service image should have:
1. **One Clear Metric** (the bigger the better): "3X Faster", "+347%", "$50K"
2. **Emotional Trigger**: Before/after, success story, or problem solved
3. **Trust Signal**: Timeframe, guarantee, or social proof badge
4. **Call-to-Context**: Not direct CTA, but implied next step

**Remember:** You're competing with Netflix thumbnails and viral memes. Your image must SCREAM value in under 3 seconds or the scroll continues.
