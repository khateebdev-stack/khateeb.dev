# Upwork Profile Strategy: The "Agency-Grade" Freelancer

## 1. The Strategy
Unlike Fiverr (which is product-based), Upwork is **relationship-based**. Clients hire YOU, not just a gig.
To win on Upwork in 2025, you must position yourself as a **"Consultant"** or **"Partner"**, not a "Worker".

**Your Positioning:**
*   **Title:** Full Stack Architect | Next.js, AI & SaaS Expert
*   **Rate:** Start at **$35-$50/hr** (Don't go lower, it looks cheap).
*   **Niche:** High-Performance SaaS & AI Applications.
