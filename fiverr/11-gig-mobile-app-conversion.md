**Don't lose customers who prefer Apps over Websites.**

Did you know that **85% of mobile time** is spent in Apps, not Browsers? If you only have a website, you are leaving money on the table.

I will convert your existing **React, Next.js, or WordPress website** into a high-performance **Mobile App (iOS & Android)**.

**What You Get:**
*   📱 **Native Feel:** It looks and feels like a real app, not just a browser tab.
*   🔔 **Push Notifications:** Send alerts directly to your users' phones (Huge for retention!).
*   ⚡ **Offline Mode:** Your app works even with bad internet connection.
*   🚀 **App Store Ready:** I handle the complex build process for Google Play & Apple App Store.

**My Tech Stack:**
*   **PWA:** Next.js PWA, Service Workers
*   **Hybrid:** Capacitor.js, React Native
*   **Tools:** Android Studio, Xcode

**Why PWA/Hybrid?**
*   **Cost Effective:** 10x cheaper than building a native app from scratch.
*   **One Codebase:** Updates to your website automatically update the app.
