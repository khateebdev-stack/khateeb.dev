## 🚀 Strategy & Outreach
The goal is to position you not just as a "freelancer" but as a **Software Architect** and **Agency-Level Developer**. We will focus on "Problem/Solution" narratives rather than just "I built this."

### **Social Media Content Calendar**

**Post 1: The "Under the Hood" Reveal**
*Hook:* "Most portfolios are just pretty pictures. Mine is a code audit."
*Body:* Share a screenshot of the "Code Spotlight" section from your Media Management Platform. Explain *why* you chose FFmpeg and Python.
*Call to Action:* "Check out the technical deep dive here: [Link]"

**Post 2: The Performance Flex**
*Hook:* "100/100 Lighthouse Scores. No compromises."
*Body:* Post the video of your "Visual Performance Cards" animating. Discuss how you achieved zero layout shift (CLS) on the Insightexus project.
*Tags:* #WebPerformance #NextJS #Engineering

**Post 3: The "Aha!" Moment**
*Hook:* "We almost failed. Here's how we fixed it."
*Body:* Share the "Breakthrough" story from the Edu NexaLearn project (The WhatsApp Bridge). People love stories about overcoming engineering hurdles.
